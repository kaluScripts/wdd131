Website Site Plan

WDD 131 Dynamic Web Fundamentals Project: Roban Stores, Awka

01 Site Name

Roban Stores, Awka

This name represents a store that provides a variety of products to our customers at fair prices in Awka, Anambra State. It is the store's real, recognizable name, so using it directly keeps the site trustworthy and easy to find for local shoppers who already know the brand.

Optional domain: robanstoresawka.com

02 Site Purpose

The site provides a catalogue of available products and the exact price to purchase each product from us. It exists so that customers can check what is in stock and what it costs before making the trip to the store, saving time and avoiding wasted visits.

  • Present an up-to-date catalogue of products sold in-store, organized by category.
  • Display clear, current pricing for each product so customers can plan a purchase confidently.
  • Give customers an easy way to contact the store with delivery requests or feedback.

03 Scenarios

Questions a typical visitor might ask when arriving at the site:

  • What are the available products in the store right now?
  • What is the exact price of an item today?
  • Do I get home delivery services, and how do I request one?
  • How do I reach the store directly if I have a question about an order?

04 Colour Schema

The palette is pulled straight from the Roban Stores mark itself, a confident red and near-black on a warm, paper-like background, with a market gold added as a pricing and hover accent, since price visibility is the whole reason this site exists.

Roban Red — #C41E2A Headings, navigation bar, buttons, links, active states
Charcoal Ink — #211C1A Body text, footer background, header background
Paper Cream — #FBF7F2 Page background, card backgrounds sit slightly lighter
Market Gold — #E8A73E Price tags, hover states, small accents and labels
Slate — #6B6560 Secondary and muted text, captions, hairline notes

05 Typography

Three fonts, each doing one job: a bold display face for headings and navigation, a clean workhorse for paragraphs, and a slab-serif reserved for prices and short tag-style labels.

Headings & Navigation

Fresh Prices, No Surprises

Poppins, 600–700 weight — used for the site name, page headings, and nav links.

Body Text

Roban Stores brings everyday groceries, household goods, and fresh produce to Awka at prices you can check before you leave home.

Karla, 400–500 weight — used for paragraphs, descriptions, and form labels.

Prices & Tags

₦1,250 / bag

Roboto Slab, 500–700 weight — used for product prices, badges, and short callouts.
